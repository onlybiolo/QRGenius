About QRGenius

QRGenius is a free online tool that allows you to generate QR codes instantly, directly in your browser.

The goal of QRGenius is to provide a fast, simple, and privacy-friendly way to create QR codes for links, text, or images without requiring registration or uploading data to a server.

How It Works

All QR codes are generated entirely on the client-side using JavaScript. This means your data never leaves your device.

Why QRGenius

  • No signup required
  • No tracking
  • Instant PNG download
  • Works on desktop and mobile

QRGenius is designed to be lightweight, fast, and accessible to everyone.
